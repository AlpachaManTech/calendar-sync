Privacy policy

Calendar Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own computer.

With the owner's consent it uses the Google Calendar scope calendar.events to show the owner's events and to save events the owner creates or edits. Calendar data stays on the owner's computer and goes only to Google. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine learning models.

Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
